# FitAssist 🏋️‍♂️

FitAssist is a modern Full-Stack application designed to help you manage your workouts, nutrition, body progress, and daily water intake all in one place.

## 🚀 Features
* **Workout Tracker:** Log your Push, Pull, and Leg routines, including sets, reps, and weights.
* **Nutrition Log:** Track your daily meals and macros (Protein, Carbs, Fats) to seamlessly reach your target goals.
* **Progress Dashboard:** Visualize your weight and body fat percentage journey over time with dynamic charts.
* **Water Tracker:** Monitor your daily water consumption against your personal targets.
* **Customizable Theme:** Personalize the application's look and feel with custom color palettes.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons, Recharts
* **Backend:** Java, Spring Boot, Spring Data JPA
* **Database:** H2 Database
